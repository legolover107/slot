# v1.15.2-legolover.1

- **Dusty Carts**: Occasionally, a cart starts with a scrambled screen instead of the game. Hold or mash **B** to blow it out, then it ejects and tries again.
- **Label Wear**: Cart labels fade, yellow, and collect scratches and worn corners as you play. Wear is tracked per cart.
- **Dry Batteries**: Occasionally, a cart's internal battery is dry. Repair it at the soldering bench to restore clock-based events.

Turn these features on or off under **Quick Menu > Gameplay > Experimental**.

This is the first release you can install from **Network > Check for Updates** on v1.15.2-pvaibhav.2. You can also copy the contents of `slot-v1.15.2-legolover.1/` from the release ZIP onto SD2.

`Cheats.pack` and `Artwork.pack` are attached and unchanged. If you don't have them yet, put them in `Cheats/` and `Labels/`. Cheats require mGBA.

See the [upstream changelog](https://github.com/pvaibhav/slot/blob/v1.15.2-pvaibhav.2/CHANGELOG.md) for upstream's changes.
